Voices of liberation

Recorded testimonies from freedom fighters, refugees, journalists, and family members who lived through the 1971 Liberation War. These interviews preserve firsthand accounts of sacrifice, resilience, and the human cost of independence.

Freedom Fighter, Mukti Bahini

Tanim Rahman

Tanim was a medical student who joined the Mukti Bahini in April 1971 and served as a field medic throughout the war. He witnessed the brutal operations and shares his experiences of caring for wounded freedom fighters in makeshift camps.

7:00
ফ
Refugee, 1971

Fatima Begum

Fatima fled Dhaka with her two children in June 1971 and spent nine months in a refugee camp in West Bengal. She recounts the harrowing journey, conditions in the camps, and the emotional reunion with her family after independence.

8:30
ড
Journalist, War Correspondent

Dipu Chakraborty

Dipu covered the Liberation War as a journalist, documenting Operation Searchlight and subsequent events. He shares insights into media coverage, the challenges of reporting during wartime, and the historic independence day celebrations.

8:00
স
Family Member, Survivor

Sabina Akhtar

Sabina's father was a freedom fighter who disappeared during the war. She speaks about the trauma of losing a parent, the search for truth about his fate, and how her family has preserved his memory across generations.

9:00
ম
Freedom Fighter, Military Sector 2

Major Mozammel Haque

Major Mozammel was a commanding officer in Sector 2, responsible for operations in the Sylhet region. He discusses military strategy, coordination with Indian forces, and the final push toward victory in December 1971.

10:00
আ
Eyewitness, Dhaka Resident

Ahmed Hossain

Ahmed was in Dhaka during Operation Searchlight and lived through the occupation. He recounts the crackdown, underground resistance networks, and the momentous day of December 16, 1971 when Pakistani forces surrendered.

7:30
