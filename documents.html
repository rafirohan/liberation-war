Declarations, treaties, and official correspondence from 1971, catalogued by type, date, and relevance. Each entry links to a detailed record with description, publication date, and source information.

Declaration of Independence

The proclamation announcing the independence of Bangladesh, issued in the early hours of 26 March 1971 and broadcast by radio from Chittagong in the days that followed.

Mujibnagar Proclamation of Independence

The formal proclamation read at Baidyanathtala, Meherpur, establishing the provisional government of Bangladesh and naming its first leadership.

Treaty9 Aug 1971

Indo-Soviet Treaty of Peace, Friendship and Cooperation

A twenty-year treaty between India and the Soviet Union, signed as regional tensions escalated ahead of the war's final phase.

Treaty16 Dec 1971

Instrument of Surrender

The document signed at the Race Course Ground in Dhaka, formalising the surrender of Pakistani forces to the Allied Command of India and Bangladesh.
