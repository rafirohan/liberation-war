Why contribute?

Preserve family heritage

Materials held by families are fragile and may be lost over time. Digitization and archival preservation ensure they survive for future generations.

Complete the historical record

Dispersed personal documents and photographs fill gaps in official histories and provide perspectives that might otherwise be lost.

Honor those who sacrifice

By sharing materials, you help memorialize the experiences and contributions of freedom fighters, refugees, and ordinary Bengalis who lived through 1971.

Support education & research

Archived materials are accessible to students, researchers, and educators, fostering deeper understanding of Bangladesh's independence struggle.

Frequently asked questions

What if I'm worried about privacy?

We respect confidentiality. Materials can be restricted from public view or made available only to verified researchers. You control the terms under which your contributions are shared.

Can I loan materials instead of donating?

Yes. We can digitize materials you loan and return the originals to you. This is an excellent option for irreplaceable family documents or photographs.

How do I know my materials will be preserved?

The archive follows international standards for digital preservation, including redundant storage, format migration, and long-term maintenance. We're committed to ensuring your contributions survive for generations.

Can I contribute photographs without text?

Absolutely. We welcome images with as much or as little accompanying information as you can provide. Even basic captions or dates help us catalog and contextualize photographs.

How long does processing take?

We process submissions on a rolling basis. Initial acknowledgment and cataloging typically occurs within 2–4 weeks. Complex or large submissions may take longer. We'll keep you updated on the status.
