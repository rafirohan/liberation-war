Nine months, in brief

Key dates of the war

A short chronology. The full, sourced timeline — with linked documents and eyewitness accounts for each date — lives on the History & Timeline page.

7 March 1971

The Race Course speech

Sheikh Mujibur Rahman addresses a mass gathering in Dhaka, calling on Bengalis to prepare for a struggle for independence.

25–26 March 1971

Operation Searchlight & the declaration

The Pakistani military launches a crackdown on Dhaka. In the early hours of 26 March, independence is declared.

17 April 1971

Formation of the Mujibnagar government

The provisional government of Bangladesh is sworn in at Baidyanathtala, in Meherpur district.

3–16 December 1971

Joint Forces offensive & victory

India enters the war alongside the Mukti Bahini. Pakistani forces in Dhaka surrender on 16 December — Bijoy Dibosh, Victory Day.
